About the company

Making regional trade connections more accessible.

Kigoma–Congo Business Link is an independent business connection focused on bringing buyers, suppliers, wholesalers and transport providers into relevant conversations across regional markets.

Who we are

A link between businesses and opportunity.

We support business-to-business connections in agricultural products, food supplies, wholesale trade and transport and logistics. Our role is to help parties identify potential contacts and begin discussions around their requirements.

We do not present ourselves as the buyer, seller, carrier or regulator in every transaction. Product quality, pricing, quantities, payment terms, permits, transport arrangements and final agreements should be verified and agreed directly by the relevant parties.

Our services

Connections that help business move forward.

We help identify relevant trade contacts and support the first steps in conversations around supply, demand and cargo movement.

↔

Buyer Connections

We help suppliers and wholesalers present their product offer to potential buyers. Useful details include product type, available volume, packaging, origin and target market.

🌾

Supplier Sourcing

We help buyers describe their sourcing requirements and seek relevant supplier contacts. Buyers should confirm specifications, samples, pricing and terms directly.

📦

Wholesale Trade Opportunities

We connect business enquiries around bulk agricultural products and food supplies. Quantities, availability and commercial terms depend on the individual supplier and transaction.

🚛

Transport & Logistics Connections

We help connect businesses with transport and logistics providers to discuss cargo size, route, loading point, destination, timing and documentation requirements.

🌍

Cross-border Trade Introductions

We support initial business conversations between parties operating in Tanzania, the DRC, Burundi and other African markets. Parties remain responsible for applicable legal and border requirements.

Enquiry Coordination

We help make enquiries clearer by gathering basic information and directing the conversation toward the relevant product, buyer, supplier or service need.
